## Context

360Score AI Predictions is my own Android app. It gives football fans AI-driven match predictions and stats.

## Problem

Fans want a quick, trustworthy read on upcoming matches without wading through betting sites and noisy forums.

## Approach

- **Designed and built the app end to end**: data pipeline, prediction display and a clean, fast Android UI.
- **Grew it through the Play Store**: store listing, screenshots and steady releases driven by reviews.
- **Kept it light** so it runs well on entry-level phones.

## Outcome

- **500K+ installs.**
- **4.5★ on Google Play.**
