## Context

Shackle builds mobile access for hotels and residences. I joined on contract as a senior Android engineer for the Dubai launch.

## Problem

Entering the Dubai market meant supporting local identity requirements, Arabic and right-to-left layouts, and accessibility, without slowing the core experience of getting through a door.

## Approach

- **Shipped mobile identity for the Dubai market**, kept simple for guests and compliant for operators.
- **Delivered full Arabic localisation and RTL**, plus accessibility across the app.
- **Integrated with hotel and lift access systems.**
- **Added in-app chat** so guests could get help without calling the front desk.

## Outcome

- **Market reach doubled** with the Dubai launch.
- **Support tickets cut 3×** after in-app chat.
