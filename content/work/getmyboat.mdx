## Context

GetMyBoat is a peer-to-peer marketplace for boat rentals and charters, with 1M+ installs. I worked as a senior Android engineer on the guest and host apps.

## Problem

People open the app at marinas and on the water, often on a weak signal. A slow cold start and screens that failed offline hurt ratings and bookings.

## Approach

- **Measured first.** Set up Macrobenchmark runs to get a reliable cold-start baseline before changing anything.
- **Cut work off the startup path**: shipped Baseline Profiles, lazily initialised SDKs and moved heavy setup off the main thread.
- **Built offline-first features** for patchy marine connectivity: cached listings and bookings in Room, queued actions and synced them when the signal came back.
- **Moved key screens to Jetpack Compose** to speed up UI work and reduce jank.

## Outcome

- **Cold start 40% faster.**
- **4.9★ Google Play rating.**
- **Retention up 25%** in key markets such as Miami.
