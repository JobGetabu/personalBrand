## Context

Sendy is an e-commerce logistics company serving riders and businesses across East and West Africa, with 1M+ people served. I was a senior mobile engineer and built the fulfilment app.

## Problem

Fulfilment ran across several countries with different networks, devices and workflows. Order cancellations were high, and the team needed to learn quickly what worked.

## Approach

- **Built the Sendy Fulfillment app from scratch**, designed for low-end devices and unreliable networks.
- **Reworked the order flow** to remove the steps that led to cancellations.
- **Ran A/B tests on onboarding** and acted on the results.
- **Set a quality bar**: 85% test coverage and crash-free rate tracked as a team metric.

## Outcome

- Used in **4+ countries**.
- **Order cancellations down 40%**, supporting GMV of up to $40M.
- **Signups up 45%** from A/B tests, with **80% retention**.
- **98% crash-free** sessions.
