## Context

Kazi connects skilled gig workers, such as cleaners, plumbers and beauticians, with customers who need a job done. I founded it and led product and engineering.

## Problem

Finding trusted, available help in Nairobi relied on word of mouth. Workers had no steady pipeline of jobs, and customers had no way to compare or book.

## Approach

- **Designed and built the platform**: customer and worker apps, matching, booking and payments.
- **Led an 8-person team** across engineering, design and operations.
- **Raised early funding** and grew the worker network city by city.

## Outcome

- **50K+ active gig workers** on the platform.
- **MTN Business App of the Year — Best African Solution, 2021.**
