## 5. Media gathering plan (how to collect safely)
1. **App screenshots:** download from the public store listings above (Play: open listing →
   screenshots; App Store: same). Use them inside the device mockup. Prefer your own
   device captures of the *public* app with a clean demo account (no real names, numbers,
   orders, or locations visible).
2. **Company logos:** use each company's official press/brand kit if published; render all
   logos monochrome in the credibility strip. Ask former employers for permission where
   there's an NDA. `TODO(verify)` per company.
3. **Press:** link to articles about the companies and awards (open in new tab). Don't
   copy article images; quote one line max with attribution.
4. **Social media:** link out to company accounts; do not embed Instagram/X widgets
   (trackers). If you want a visual, screenshot a public post, crop to content, strip
   metadata, self-host.
5. **Talks/community photos:** only group photos where you have rights and no minors or
   private individuals are identifiable without consent.
6. **Strip EXIF** on everything before committing.
